Madara Lazdāne I choose both, too…

There's a myth I no longer believe. That a person has to choose just one path.

One career. One profession. One thing you're good at.

At some point I realised I don't have to choose. And maybe you don't either.

I still work a salaried job, and at the same time I build my own projects, write, create content, work with Fintech companies and run career coaching sessions. And I don't feel that these worlds compete. Quite the opposite. They complement each other.

My job teaches me system, discipline, responsibility and collaboration. My personal projects give me creativity, courage and the feeling that I'm shaping my own life rather than just completing tasks. Each experience gives me something the other can't. And this experience has become the foundation of how I work with people.

My professional background is in law and sustainability management in real estate. I like structure. I like understanding how things work and finding solutions. I like numbers, systems and clear next steps. But I love writing just as much — creating content, organising women's networking events and developing ideas.

You don't have to give up one part of your life to make room for another.

Burnout doesn't always come from doing too much.

Sometimes it comes from doing, for too long, what no longer fits our values, our potential and the life we actually want. That's why the question "What do you want to do?" doesn't really tell you much. Try finding answers to these instead:

  1. What kind of life do you want to build for yourself?
  2. How much time do you have for it?
  3. How much energy?
  4. What are you ready to invest?
  5. And how do you turn your idea into something real without leaving everything else behind?

From a free idea to something of my own.

My personal projects didn't start with a business plan either. They started with things I simply enjoyed doing. Writing. Creating content. Helping others find a job and sort out their LinkedIn profile. Talking about careers and personal growth. At first I didn't charge for any of it — I just did it. And over time these interests turned into new projects, collaborations, income streams and people in my life.

Today, alongside my main job, I write for a women's magazine, work with companies in the fintech industry on communication and content projects, build LinkedIn strategies for companies and personal brands, and run individual growth and career coaching sessions.

And yes, part of it is about extra income. But not only. It's also about getting to use different sides of myself. About new conversations, people and hobbies. About the feeling that my world is getting bigger.

A few things I'd say over a cup of coffee.

  1. 01.

    You don't have to choose just one path.

    A career and something of your own can grow in parallel. You can be good at your job and at the same time build your brand, project or business idea.

  2. 02.

    Your interests aren't random.

    What you do in your free time, what you love talking about, or what people often ask you to help with can be more than a hobby. Maybe your next idea is already there.

  3. 03.

    Money is part of the conversation.

    Your idea has a place in your budget too. We look not only at what you want to create, but also at what it costs, how much time it takes and how it can become financially meaningful.

  4. 04.

    Start with what you already have.

    You don't need a perfect plan. You already have experience, skills, contacts, interests and knowledge. Start with those. The first step is often much smaller than it seems at first.
